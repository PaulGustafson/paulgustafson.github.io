4. Hermite Functions & Central phase-space translations &
Stone--von Neumann decomposition and the Mehler kernel & Oscillator spectroscopy\\
5. Determinantal Kernels & $U(W)$ acting on Hermitian endomorphisms &
Orthogonal-polynomial, sine, and Airy kernels & Quantum-chaotic spectra\\
6. Bessel Functions & Euclidean, Poincar\'e, and radial rotation symmetry &
Hankel, mass-shell, and partial-wave decompositions & Diffraction, correlations, and hard-sphere scattering\\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{II. Hypergeometric Functions}}\\
7. Gauss Hypergeometric Functions & Asymptotic translations of the line &
Jost decomposition and Gauss connection formulas & Reflectionless transmission\\
8. Legendre Functions & $SL_2(\mathbb R)$ acting on $\mathbb H$ &
Unitary dual and Mehler--Fock decomposition & Hyperbolic-drum spectra\\
9. Kummer Functions & $SO(4)$ dynamical symmetry &
Kummer, Laguerre, and Coulomb spectral functions & Hydrogen and Rutherford observables\\
10. Heun Functions & $\mathbb R\times SO(3)$ acting on Schwarzschild spacetime &
Regge--Wheeler, Heun, and parabolic-cylinder modes & Scalar ringdown\\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{III. Character Theory}}\\
11. Weyl Characters & Weyl symmetry of reductive weight spaces &
Highest-weight, Weyl-character, and Schur decomposition & Flavor multiplets\\
12. Automorphic Forms & $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ acting on $\mathbb H$ &
Eisenstein, theta, eta, and automorphic spectral decomposition & Cusp scattering\\
13. Virasoro Characters & $\operatorname{Diff}^+(S^1)$ and Ising spin reversal &
Virasoro, Toeplitz, and Ising character decompositions & Critical and finite-size spectra\\
14. Affine Characters & $LK_c\rtimes S^1$ acting on loop fields &
Weyl--Kac characters and KZ conformal blocks & WZW finite-size spectra\\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{IV. $q$-Series}}\\
15. Basic Hypergeometric Functions & $U_v(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ on tensor products &
Quantum Peter--Weyl, $q$-Hahn, and $q$-Racah functions & XXZ spectroscopy\\
16. Jack Polynomials & Reflection groups acting on Euclidean space &
Dunkl, generalized-Bessel, and Jack decomposition & Calogero--Sutherland spectroscopy\\
17. Macdonald Polynomials & DAHA acting on an algebraic torus &
Cherednik, Macdonald, and Askey--Wilson decomposition & Ruijsenaars spectrum\\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{V. Algebraic Geometry}}\\
18. Elliptic Functions & Elliptic translations on complex tori &
Abel--Jacobi inversion and elliptic functions & Nonlinear-pendulum periods\\
19. Bergman Kernels & $\operatorname{Aut}(X,L)$ acting on sections &
Riemann--Roch, localization, and Bergman projection & Landau-level density\\
20. Baker--Akhiezer Functions & Translations and KdV flows on periodic potentials &
Theta, Baker--Akhiezer, and Bloch--Floquet decomposition & Band-gap spectroscopy\\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{VI. Algebraic Topology}}\\
21. Monopole Harmonics & Gauge symmetry of connections and coupled operators &
Index decomposition and monopole harmonics & Integer Hall conductance\\
22. Eta Functions & Frame symmetry and tangential structures &
Pontryagin--Thom and eta-spectral decomposition & Invertible-phase response\\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{VII. Tensor Categories}}\\
23. Hall--Littlewood Polynomials & $G[[z]]$ acting on affine-Grassmannian modifications &
Satake convolution and Hall--Littlewood decomposition & 't Hooft fusion\\
24. Modular $S$-Matrices & Fusion categories acting on fusion state spaces &
Modular sine transform and Verlinde decomposition & Anyon interferometry\\
